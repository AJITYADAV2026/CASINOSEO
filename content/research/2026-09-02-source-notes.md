## Source 1 — Macau September Revenue Outlook

**Source:** GGRAsia, “Macau casino GGR tipped for September rebound after soft August: analysts,” published 2 September 2026.

**URL:** https://www.ggrasia.com/macau-casino-ggr-tipped-for-september-rebound-after-soft-august-analysts

Macau casino gross gaming revenue was forecast to return to year-on-year growth in September 2026. JP Morgan Securities forecast September GGR of MOP19.64 billion, approximately 7% higher year on year and equivalent to 89% of the September 2019 level. Seaport Research Partners forecast approximately MOP20.12 billion, or 10% growth year on year.

The article contextualized those forecasts against August 2026 market-wide GGR of MOP21.89 billion, down 1.2% year on year but up 8.1% sequentially from July. JP Morgan characterized the sequential improvement as more reflective of a weak July than a strong August. The brokerage maintained its full-year 2026 Macau GGR forecast of MOP254.28 billion, representing 3% year-on-year growth.

Named analyst sources included Daniel Politzer, Samuel Nielsen, and Michael Hirsh of JP Morgan, and Vitaly Umansky of Seaport Research Partners. The article also cited Macau’s Gaming Inspection and Coordination Bureau for the August market-wide figure.

## Source 2 — Paradise Co August Casino Sales

**Source:** GGRAsia, “Paradise Co sees August casino sales jump 32pct y-o-y to US$77mln, with growth across all four venues,” published 2 September 2026.

**URL:** https://www.ggrasia.com/paradise-co-sees-august-casino-sales-jump-32pct-y-o-y-to-us77mln-with-growth-across-all-four-venues

Paradise Co reported August casino sales of nearly KRW104.91 billion, approximately US$76.8 million, representing 32.1% year-on-year growth and 35.8% sequential growth from July. The report was based on a company filing lodged with the Korea Exchange and supplementary business data published on the same day.

Table-game sales reached KRW99.43 billion, up 32.7% year on year and 36.6% month on month. Machine-game sales were approximately KRW5.47 billion, up 23.1% year on year and 23.4% month on month. Paradise City in Incheon contributed nearly KRW58.79 billion, up 29.8% year on year.

For the first eight months of 2026, aggregate Paradise Co casino sales reached KRW658.50 billion, up 8.1% from the comparable 2025 period. August casino drop was KRW680.50 billion, up 3.7% year on year, while year-to-date drop reached nearly KRW5.17 trillion, up 8.3%.

## Source 3 — New Jersey Supreme Court Petition on Prediction Markets

**Source:** New Jersey Office of the Attorney General, “AG Davenport: SCOTUS Must Resolve States’ Authority To Regulate Sports Gambling on Prediction Markets,” released 2 September 2026.

**URL:** https://www.njoag.gov/ag-davenport-scotus-must-resolve-states-authority-to-regulate-sports-gambling-on-prediction-markets/

New Jersey announced a petition asking the U.S. Supreme Court to review the extent of state authority over sports-related prediction markets. The Attorney General’s office argued that a divided Third Circuit ruling threatened state regulatory power and could create broad consequences for sports wagering conducted both through online operators and at brick-and-mortar casinos.

The release framed state gambling laws as safeguards for minors, problem-gambling mitigation, prevention of insider trading, and operator financial stability. It also connected the dispute to the Supreme Court’s earlier decision in *Murphy v. NCAA*, in which the Court recognized that states could decide whether to allow sports wagering where Congress had not directly regulated it.

This source is an official statement from one litigating party and should therefore be presented as New Jersey’s legal position, not as a neutral prediction of the Supreme Court’s eventual decision.

## Source 4 — Professional Market Makers in Prediction Exchanges

**Source:** iGaming Business, Scott Longley, “Meet the invisible sportsbook powering the prediction market boom,” published 2 September 2026.

**URL:** https://igamingbusiness.com/prediction-markets/meet-sportsboo-white-swan-prediction-market/

The article reported that professional firms are increasingly providing pricing, liquidity, and counterparty capacity on prediction exchanges that are often described as peer-to-peer markets. Bernard Marantelli, founder of White Swan Data, described Kalshi as structurally similar to a sportsbook without an in-house risk team and said White Swan represented as much as 40% of activity on some secondary platforms.

The report identified White Swan and Susquehanna as operating at industrial scale in parlay-related markets, with Jump Trading, Mojo, and DL Trading cited among other possible major participants. Marantelli said Kalshi’s client count grew fivefold during the World Cup and forecast that NFL prediction markets could generate between US$5 billion and US$7 billion in weekly liability.

The quantitative claims in this article are attributed to an industry participant and should be labeled as company estimates rather than independently verified market totals.

## Source 5 — RGB Machine Sales and Digital Expansion Outlook

**Source:** GGRAsia, “RGB expects stronger 2H, eyes Macau machine replacement demand: report,” published 2 September 2026.

**URL:** https://www.ggrasia.com/rgb-expects-stronger-2h-eyes-macau-machine-replacement-demand-report

RGB targeted delivery of the remaining 1,700 gaming machines under a 3,000-unit 2026 sales goal. Management cited potential demand from integrated resorts in the Philippines and from an electronic-gaming-machine replacement cycle in Macau tied to updated technical standards.

RGB’s leisure chief operating officer, Chuah Eng Meng, said the company had submitted a tender to a major Macau casino, with a third-quarter decision and possible fourth-quarter delivery. The relevant casino’s replacement programme was expected to proceed over one to two years.

RGB reported a 26.3% year-on-year decline in second-quarter shareholder profit to MYR10.3 million, while revenue rose 15.5% to MYR109.6 million. Sales-and-marketing revenue increased 20.1% to MYR93.1 million, although segment profit before tax fell 21.1%.

The company was also pursuing digital gaming services in the Philippines, including content, aggregation, and managed white-label services. Management said a proposed white-label arrangement could generate a 20%–30% revenue share, but the associated bids and discussions were not yet completed.

## Source 6 — Philippines Considers Wider Visa-Free Entry for Chinese Visitors

**Source:** GGRAsia, “Philippines eyes visa-free entry for Chinese to resort hub of Clark, other holiday hotspots: tourism secretary,” published 2 September 2026.

**URL:** https://www.ggrasia.com/philippines-eyes-visa-free-entry-for-chinese-to-resort-hub-of-clark-other-holiday-hotspots-tourism-secretary

Philippine tourism secretary Dita Angara-Mathay said the government was working to expand visa-free entry for Chinese-mainland passport holders beyond Cebu and Manila to Bohol, Palawan, Clark, and Caticlan. The proposal remained subject to broader government approval.

The Department of Tourism reported 4.11 million foreign arrivals from January through August 2026, up 3.7% year on year. Among the ten largest source markets, arrivals from China increased nearly 69.2% and arrivals from India increased 32.2%.

Clark is both a major air hub and a resort market containing casino facilities, making the proposed visa arrangement relevant to integrated-resort visitation. The final digest should characterize this as a tourism-policy proposal rather than an approved or implemented rule.

## Source 7 — Independent Cross-Check of Paradise Co Results

**Source:** Asia Gaming Brief, Viviana Chan, “Paradise Co. August casino revenue reaches record $76.9M despite flat VIP traffic,” published 2 September 2026 and updated 3 September 2026.

**URL:** https://agbrief.com/news/south-korea/02/09/2026/paradise-co-august-casino-revenue-reaches-record-76-9m-despite-flat-vip-traffic/

Asia Gaming Brief independently reported the same headline revenue, year-on-year growth, month-on-month growth, table revenue, table drop, machine revenue, and year-to-date totals cited by GGRAsia, attributing the data to a Korea Exchange filing and Paradise Co’s monthly operating data.

The article added context that August revenue exceeded the previous monthly record of KRW98.14 billion set in May. VIP visiting days were broadly flat year on year at 16,950 versus 16,879, while table hold increased to 14.6% from 11.4% a year earlier and 10.8% in July. This suggests that the revenue jump reflected both activity and a materially stronger hold rate rather than a comparable increase in VIP visitation.
