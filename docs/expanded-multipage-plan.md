# CasinoVerse Expanded Multi-Page Plan

## Product Position

CasinoVerse remains an **informational editorial publication**, not a gambling product. The expanded site combines casino history, game literacy, destination context, architecture, culture, sourced facts, video-editorial planning, and daily industry research without betting, deposits, bonuses, payments, wallets, or playable real-money games.

## Navigation Model

The desktop primary navigation will include **Articles, Games, History, Culture, Destinations, Vlogs, Facts, Gallery, and About**, with Search as an icon and Home available through the wordmark. The mobile drawer will include the same routes plus Guides, Archive, and Responsible Entertainment. Privacy, Disclaimer, Terms, Cookie Settings, Editorial Standards, Contact, and the research archive belong in the footer.

## New Content Responsibilities

The Articles index reuses real database stories and will not fabricate popularity metrics. “Popular” will be presented as an **Editor’s selection** unless measured analytics are available. The Vlogs page will not fabricate episode titles, view counts, durations, hosts, or published videos; it will explain planned formats and route readers to source-based written reporting until genuine CasinoVerse media exists.

Individual game pages will distinguish rules from strategy claims, identify common regional variations, show probability or house-edge context where reliably sourced, and repeat the responsible-entertainment boundary. History, Culture, Destinations, and Facts pages require direct authoritative references and careful language around uncertain or disputed historical claims.

The Gallery will use only newly generated publication-owned visual assets and descriptive editorial captions. It will not present generated imagery as documentary evidence; each gallery item will be labeled **Editorial illustration**.

## Newsletter Scope

The homepage newsletter form will store a normalized email, affirmative editorial-newsletter consent timestamp, status, and audit timestamps. Duplicate active subscriptions will return a clear idempotent success; invalid emails or missing consent will be rejected. No promotional gambling offers will be sent, and no third-party email provider will be integrated without separate authorization.

## SEO and Sitemap

Every new indexable route receives a unique title, description, canonical URL, social metadata, one H1, and server-rendered substantive content. Static editorial pages and five game-detail pages will be added to the dynamic sitemap. Search remains `noindex`. Legal pages may be indexed but will not be included in the news sitemap. No Search Console indexing operation is performed while Agent 4 remains paused.

## Visual Assignment Requirement

Every new page family receives a unique hero. Each game detail receives a distinct game-specific visual. History, Culture, Destinations, Vlogs, Facts, and Gallery also receive separate image collections so no hero or card image repeats across page families.
