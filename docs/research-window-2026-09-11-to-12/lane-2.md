### 1. CCI Closes Antitrust Probe Against Google Over Real-Money Gaming
**Facts:** The Competition Commission of India (CCI) closed its antitrust investigation against Google regarding its Play Store and Google Ads policies for real-money gaming (RMG) applications. The case, originally filed by WinZO Games in 2022, alleged that Google favored Daily Fantasy Sports and Rummy apps while excluding other RMG apps. The CCI closed the case following the enactment of the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025, which banned online money games, advertising, and related payment facilitation. The CCI noted that the new law left no lawful RMG market for Google to selectively permit or restrict.
**Why it is material:** This represents a significant regulatory and legal development in the Indian online gaming sector, directly resulting from the new federal ban on real-money gaming. It concludes a major antitrust case involving a global tech giant and a leading Indian gaming company.

### 2. Haryana Government Notifies Rummy and Poker as 'Games of Skill'
**Facts:** The Haryana Government issued a notification on September 10, 2026, declaring the card games Rummy and Poker as 'games of skill' under the Haryana Prevention of Public Gambling Act, 2025. The notification was issued by Additional Chief Secretary (Home) Sudhir Rajpal. This removes them from the traditional category of gambling, provided they are played for amusement.
**Why it is material:** This is a state-level regulatory clarification that distinguishes skill-based games from games of chance under state gambling laws, impacting the legal status of these games within Haryana.

### 3. Online Gaming Promotion and Regulation Rules, 2026 Come Into Force
**Facts:** The Online Gaming Promotion and Regulation Rules, 2026, came into force. These rules implement a complete ban on online real-money games, including those based on chance, skill, or a mix of both. The rules also prohibit advertising and financial transactions related to these platforms. The rules establish the Indian Online Gaming Authority under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology as a unified regulator.
**Why it is material:** This is the enactment of major federal regulations fundamentally altering the legal landscape for online gaming in India, establishing a new regulatory body and enforcing a nationwide ban on real-money gaming.
