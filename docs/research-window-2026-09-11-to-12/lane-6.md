### 1. New Zealand Publishes Online Casino Gambling Regulations 2026 and Confirms Auction Format

New Zealand's Department of Internal Affairs (DIA) has officially confirmed that the upcoming online casino licenses will be allocated via an ascending clock auction with sealed bids. In conjunction with this announcement, the DIA published the Online Casino Gambling Regulations 2026, which establish strict harm minimization and consumer protection standards for the new market. Applications for these licenses will close on December 1, 2026, with the official market launch targeted for 2027. Furthermore, existing advertising restrictions will remain firmly in place until the market officially goes live in 2027.

This development is highly material as it represents a major regulatory milestone in New Zealand's transition from an unregulated offshore model to a fully regulated domestic online casino market. It details the exact licensing mechanism (ascending clock auction) and officially enacts the regulations that will govern consumer protection, harm minimization, and advertising. It is important to note that the market launch is targeted for 2027, meaning these are enacted regulations preparing for a future go-live date, rather than an immediate market opening.
