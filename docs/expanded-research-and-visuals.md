## Editorial Cautions

Game origins are often evolutionary rather than attributable to one inventor. The pages will label disputed origin stories and avoid presenting folklore as settled history. Probability figures will be tied to stated rules and will never be presented as a winning method. Market statistics will retain their year and jurisdiction. AI-generated visual assets will be labeled **Editorial illustration** wherever documentary confusion is possible.
