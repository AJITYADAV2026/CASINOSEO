## Editorial Desk Matrix

Each year will be checked across **industry and regulation**, **casino operations and technology**, **games and game literacy**, **places, architecture, and destinations**, **culture and media**, and **responsible play and gambling harm**. Not every year requires a record in every desk; records will be included only when a material, verifiable development exists. The minimum acceptance rule is at least one verified database record for every calendar year, with multi-desk coverage across the complete period.
