## Coverage Notes
Research into the 2024 casino industry uncovered significant developments across multiple desks, primarily driven by regulatory actions and market expansion. The `industry_and_regulation` desk is well-represented, with major milestones including Pennsylvania crossing the $6 billion gaming revenue mark for the first time in a calendar year and the Illinois Gaming Board issuing a new casino owners' license to Wind Creek Chicago Southland. The `operations_and_technology` desk saw regulatory changes, such as Ohio amending rules to remove credit cards as a funding option for deposit-enabled sports gaming accounts. Evidence relies on official government press releases and state commission documents. Date precision varies from exact days for specific regulatory meetings to the full year for annual revenue reports.
