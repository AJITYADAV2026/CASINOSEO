## Coverage Notes
Research for 2011 focuses heavily on the `industry_and_regulation` and `operations_and_technology` desks due to major structural shifts in the US market. The year was defined by the DOJ's "Black Friday" crackdown on unregulated online poker and its subsequent December reversal on the Wire Act, which laid the legal groundwork for future regulated online platforms like CasinoVerse. Exact dates were verified for regulatory actions, while some operational events were limited to year-level precision. Evidence is drawn from reputable news, trade publications, and official regulators.
