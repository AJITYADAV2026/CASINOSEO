# CasinoVerse Casino-Editorial Redesign Brief

## Research Synthesis

Casino trade publications organize coverage around **latest news, current issues, features, design, supplier technology, company developments, and expert voices**, while hospitality education separates the industry into **gaming operations, regulatory compliance, security and surveillance, resort management, entertainment, technology, and game mechanics**.[1] [2] Contemporary casino architecture increasingly combines theatrical focal points with organized circulation, mixed-use amenities, local storytelling, sustainability, and calmer zones rather than treating the casino as a single undifferentiated gaming floor.[3]

Responsible-gambling information must remain a first-class desk, not a footer afterthought. Current regulatory frameworks commonly address self-exclusion, visible disclosures, advertising restrictions, time or wager limits, credit and payment restrictions, employee training, and treatment or research funding.[4] Recent field research also found that access to responsible-gambling information can be inconsistent, strengthening the case for a dedicated, plainly labeled CasinoVerse safety section with direct resources.[5]

## Navigation Decision

The supplied navigation reference is clean but visually generic. CasinoVerse will keep its restrained dark editorial character while introducing a two-level information architecture that feels specific to casino reporting.

| Navigation level | Labels | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary publication nav | **Blog**, **Casino Floor**, **Industry**, **Places & Design**, **Research**, **Vlog** | Short, high-intent entry points that map the casino as a physical system and an industry. |
| Secondary casino rail | **Games**, **History**, **Culture**, **Destinations**, **Facts**, **Gallery**, **Guides**, **Responsible** | Preserves every existing route while reducing crowding and improving discoverability. |
| Utility | Search, About, latest research edition | Keeps publication tools visible without competing with the main topic hierarchy. |

## Homepage Section Architecture

| Section | Editorial job | Casino-specific visual cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Opening Table** | Lead Blog analysis and the clearest current story. | Overhead green-felt table, roulette geometry, dealer shoe, source documents. |
| **Floor Report** | Latest daily developments from the three-agent workflow. | Casino pit plan, timestamp rail, source markers. |
| **Inside the House** | Operations, surveillance, technology, suppliers, and resort systems. | Empty pit, surveillance wall, cage and slot-mechanism details. |
| **Rules of Play** | Regulation, licensing, compliance, and public policy. | Licensing dossier, gaming-control seal forms, chip trays as evidence markers. |
| **Game Laboratory** | Poker, blackjack, roulette, baccarat, and slot mechanics. | Distinct table layouts, probability diagrams, wheel and card construction. |
| **Places & Design** | History, culture, architecture, destinations, and entertainment. | Casino façades, theatres, circulation plans, archival interiors. |
| **Responsible Play Desk** | Harm, limits, self-exclusion, support, and policy evidence. | Clock, limit card, quiet exit route, support folder—never winning imagery. |
| **Research Vault** | Dated digests, facts, methods, sources, and corrections. | Casino ledger, archival chips, source index, date-stamped files. |
| **Vlog Studio** | Genuine video-led reporting only. | Camera, field recorder, caption transcript, empty casino set. |

## Visual System

The revised palette will use **casino green felt**, **oxblood/burgundy**, **antique brass**, **ivory**, and **near-black**. Visual geometry will borrow from roulette rings, card corners, chip edges, table-layout rules, brass plaques, and surveillance grids. Images will show recognizable casino environments and objects, but the compositions will remain unoccupied, research-led, and free from money piles, celebratory winners, bonus language, betting prompts, or glamour-only spectacle.

Every major page and Blog story will receive a distinct image. Heroes will reserve text-safe negative space; card images will use closer object studies. Alt text will describe the visible casino environment and the evidence relationship rather than making unverifiable documentary claims.
