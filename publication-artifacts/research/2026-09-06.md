## 1. Pennsylvania veterans’ group presses lawmakers on electronic charitable gaming

The Morning Call published a contributed opinion from Pennsylvania VFW state commander James Brown urging lawmakers to amend the state’s small-games-of-chance framework to permit electronic pull-tabs.[1] This is an **advocacy proposal**, not an enacted policy change. The article connects the proposal to the Pennsylvania Supreme Court’s June ruling that the skill-game devices before it qualify as slot machines under state law and to the court’s temporary enforcement stay.
